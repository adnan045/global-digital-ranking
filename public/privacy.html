Transparency matters.

We want people to know what information they share, why it is used and how they can contact us about it.

Last updated: 4 August 2026

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, work email, company, website, service interest and any message you choose to send. We may also receive basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, prepare an audit or proposal, manage the relationship and keep records of the conversation. We do not sell enquiry details.

Where it is stored

Lead information may be stored in the website CRM and in service providers used to operate the website, email and analytics. Configure your actual providers and locations before publishing this notice.

Email and marketing

We will not add you to a marketing list without an appropriate lawful basis and clear information. Every marketing message should provide a clear way to opt out. You can contact us at hello@globaldigitalranking.com.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete information we hold about you, subject to applicable law. You can also object to direct marketing at any time.
